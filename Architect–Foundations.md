# Introduction to AI Fluency
AI Fluency means interacting with AI system in ways that are Effective, Efficient, Ethical and Safe.

## AI Fluency Framework
1. Delegation: When should humans do the work and when should AI?
2. Description: How do we communicate clearly with AI systems?
3. Discernment: How do we evaluate what AI gives us?
4. Diligence: How to ensure the integration with AI is responsible transparent and accountable?

---

## 3 Main ways people engage with AI
- Automation : AI does the work for you, explicit instructions needed from human.
- Augmentation : You and AI collaboratively work together.
- Agency: AI work independently on users behalf.

Human Provides: Critical Thinking, Judgement, Creativity & Ethical Oversight.
AI Provides: Speed, Scale, Pattern Recognition & Processing abilities.

---

## Delegation
1. Understand the problem you are trying to solve.
2. Understand the capabilities of available AI tools.
3. Break Complex work into smaller parts.
4. Deciding who does what(Human and Claude).
5. Choosing the right mode of interaction: Automation, Augmentation and Agency.

### Platform Awareness
A working knowledge of available AI systems and their specific capabilities and limitations.


### Task Delegation
The strategic process of dividing work between humans and AI. Ask the below Questions
1. What could be usefully automated?
2. Where would augmentation create more value than working separately?
3. What should be done by human alone?
4. What could be done by agent on your behalf.

---

## Description

### Product Description:
- Clearly describe the goal
- Context, format, audience style and other constraints
- Give AI the info it needs to deliver the goal, instead of guessing.

### Process Description: Guiding the AI thought process.
- Provide training specific to your problem.
- Specific data, key tasks, preferred order.


### Performance Description
- AI tools are interactive systems that can behave differently in different contexts.
- We need to explain how AI should behave to get the best results.
